## The work, in `server.py` and the HTTP half of `config.py`

- `StaticTokenVerifier` built from **`JOBVITE_HTTP_TOKENS`** at startup.
- `require_scopes` on the **three data classes of §4.1** - candidate PII, public job data, job feed -
  with scope names drawn from that map.
- `allowed_hosts` / `allowed_origins` **whenever `JOBVITE_MCP_HOST` is not loopback**.
- `RateLimitingMiddleware` with a **mandatory** `get_client_id`.
- `TimingMiddleware`, and `StructuredLoggingMiddleware` with **`include_payloads=False`**.
- Inbound `X-Request-ID` **validated as a UUIDv4 before use** and echoed.

**The three variables already exist and were named by the design, not invented.** `JOBVITE_MCP_HOST`
(`127.0.0.1`), `JOBVITE_MCP_PORT` (`8000`), and secret-class `JOBVITE_HTTP_TOKENS`. An earlier draft
of this unit was **unbuildable** because none of them existed; a reviewer guessed names that happened
to match and they were correctly NOT adopted on that basis. **Naming them was the design's call.**

## The verification bar, and one item is the whole unit's integrity

**FIVE ABSENCES ASSERTED AGAINST A STACK NEVER PROVEN NON-EMPTY CANNOT TELL "EXCLUDED" FROM "NO
MIDDLEWARE AT ALL".** `ResponseCaching`, `ErrorHandling`, `ResponseLimiting`, `Retry` and `Ping` must
be **absent**, each excluded for a measured reason (ADR-0004, `DESIGN.md:1177-1202`), and re-adding
one is a silent regression.

**So the positive control is not optional and it is what gives those five meaning:** assert the three
ADOPTED middleware are PRESENT in the constructed stack, and that `StructuredLoggingMiddleware`
carries `include_payloads=False` - a threat row exists for that value (C2-I1, `DESIGN.md:1732`).
Draft 3 positively verified only `RateLimitingMiddleware`, leaving `Timing` and `StructuredLogging`
with no assertion at all.

Also required:

- **A token lacking a scope: the tool is ABSENT from `tools/list`** and a direct call returns
  "Unknown tool", **not** a permission error. That is confusing-but-correct and the README must
  document it.
- **Two differently scoped tokens see different tool sets.**
- **Rate limiting is PER CLIENT, not the framework default.** Two clients; one drains its bucket,
  the other is unaffected. The default keys everyone to the literal `"global"`
  (`FASTMCP-SPIKE-4.md:898`), which is the defect this assertion exists for.
- A malformed inbound `X-Request-ID` (newlines, over-long) is **replaced rather than used** - C7-T1.
  `resolve_request_id` already does this; assert the transport path reaches it.
- **`JOBVITE_HTTP_TOKENS` unset with `JOBVITE_MCP_TRANSPORT=http` fails AT STARTUP naming the
  variable**, and does not start an unauthenticated server. **Positive control**: a well-formed token
  map starts and its tokens authenticate.
- `JOBVITE_MCP_PORT` and `JOBVITE_MCP_HOST` are honoured, and the non-loopback path **sets**
  `allowed_hosts`/`allowed_origins` rather than leaving them at the default.
