## The work, all in `services/jobvite_client.py`

- **Every scan starts at `start=0`.** `DESIGN.md:455-464`. This is the whole mechanism and it is one
  character. Do not "improve" it to 1.
- Page cap **500 on v2**, **1000 on `/v1/jobFeed`**.
- A **per-scan seen set** on returned ids, dropping duplicates from a clamped or overlapping page.
- **Termination on a SHORT page** - `len(items) < count` - and **never on `total`**.
- **The completeness check against `total` fires ONLY on an exhaustive scan.** `DESIGN.md:469-477`.
- The start base is **per-resource, not global**, with `JOBVITE_PAGINATION_START_BASE` as an override.
- `min(transport_cap, configured_result_cap)`, where the configured half is `JOBVITE_MAX_RESULTS`,
  default **50** (`DESIGN.md:1572-1575`).

## The three traps in this unit, which is most of why it has a brief

**1. De-duplication is a defence against OVER-reading only.** `DESIGN.md:465-468` is explicit: it
cannot recover a record that was never returned, *"which is exactly why the fix is starting at 0
rather than de-duplicating harder"*. **Write a test that proves the limitation**, not just the
behaviour - a scan that never returns record zero must still come back short, and the suite must say
so. A test that only shows duplicates being dropped lets a future author conclude the seen set is the
safety mechanism and move the start.

**2. The completeness check has TWO required arms and the second is the one people skip.** It must
fire on an exhaustive scan with a missing record, **and must NOT fire on a capped call** - a capped
call reports `showing 50 of 1,240` and is not an anomaly. `DESIGN.md:474` says wiring the check to
every call *"would fire the alarm on the default path and train everyone to ignore it"*. **Both arms
are required.** A single-armed test passes on an implementation that alarms on everything.

**3. THE RESULT CAP IS ONE BEHAVIOUR SPLIT ACROSS TWO FILES AND YOU OWN ONLY HALF.** U5 built the
in-tool half in `tools/jobs.py`. You add the transport half and the `min()` that composes them. The
plan says it directly: *"neither unit may assume it owns all of it."* Do not reimplement U5's
reporting string, and do not edit `tools/jobs.py` - if the composition needs something from it, say
so in the report and I will route it.
