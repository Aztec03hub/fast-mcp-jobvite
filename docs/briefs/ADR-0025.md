### Q1 IS WITHDRAWN. Not deferred, not applied. Withdrawn.

ADR-0025's Q1 ruling says an exhaustive scan should use the **raw transport cap**, and gives as its
reason that *"the design stated no policy and an implementation must not invent one"*.

**That reason is measurably false.** `git show 8a9d63c:docs/DESIGN.md`, §4.5, whole sentence:

> "Offset-based, `start` and `count`. Page cap **500** on v2, **1000** on `/v1/jobFeed`. These are
> the *transport* limits. The *result* limit returned to a model is separate and configurable
> (§7.7); the two are related by `min(transport_cap, configured_result_cap)`."

The design states a paging policy, and `services/jobvite_client.py`'s `scan()` implements exactly
it. U6's deleted branch was not filling a vacuum - it was contradicting §4.5. A ruling resting on a
false reading of the design gets withdrawn, not applied. **There is no defect here to fix.**
